I am a Master’s student in Computer Science at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/en/) and a Research Assistant at the [ETH/EPFL AI Center](https://ai.epfl.ch/) ([Swiss AI Initiative](https://www.swiss-ai.org/)), where I work on inference and infrastructure for the [Apertus](https://www.swiss-ai.org/apertus) LLM project.

I recently completed a six-month internship at [Oracle](https://labs.oracle.com/), where I worked on [AgentSpec](https://github.com/oracle/agent-spec) and [WayFlow](https://github.com/oracle/wayflow), Oracle’s open-source libraries for building agentic workflows. Previously, I completed a semester research project at [INDY](https://indy.epfl.ch/) at EPFL on Bayesian Optimization, which resulted in a publication at [ICLR 2026](https://openreview.net/forum?id=zUbBaWAM1Q). Before joining EPFL, I earned a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering from [Sharif University of Technology](https://en.sharif.edu/), where I completed my thesis on graph mining in social networks.

## Research Interests

In a nutshell, I am interested in **Machine Learning**, with a particular focus on the following areas:

* **LLMs & Agents:** During my internship at Oracle, I worked on various aspects of building, using, and **evaluating** LLMs and agents, with a particular focus on **reasoning capabilities**.
* **LLM Inference & Infrastructure:** At the ETH/EPFL AI Center, I work on **high-performance LLM serving and deployment infrastructure** for the Apertus LLM project, running on the CSCS Alps supercomputing infrastructure.
* **Bayesian Optimization & Probabilistic Models:** During a semester research project at EPFL, I worked on incorporating **invariance into Bayesian Optimization** to improve sample efficiency and achieve lower regret bounds, resulting in a publication at **ICLR 2026**. During my Bachelor’s, I worked on **graph mining for social networks**, with a focus on applying probabilistic models to time-series data to uncover hidden spatial and temporal relationships in social media, resulting in publications at **arXiv (2023)** and **Scientific Reports (2024)**.
