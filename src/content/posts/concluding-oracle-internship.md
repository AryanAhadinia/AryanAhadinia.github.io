Happy to conclude my six month internship at [Oracle](https://oracle.com/)!

During my internship, I designed and implemented the evaluation package of [Open Agent Specification](https://github.com/oracle/agent-spec), an extension that standardizes how agentic systems are evaluated in a framework-agnostic way.

I also worked on features of [WayFlow](https://github.com/oracle/wayflow) (wayflowcore) and pyagentspec. After they were open-sourced, I contributed [VariableStep](https://github.com/oracle/wayflow/pull/24), a single step that writes and then reads one or more flow variables, replacing the separate VariableReadStep and VariableWriteStep that made flows verbose and hard to read.
